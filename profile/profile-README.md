## Who's up here 🎃

One keeper and one night owl, building a storm cloud of our own: practical tools anyone can use, an open curriculum anyone can learn from, and security research that makes the web harder to break — never the other way around.

**The mission:** keep the world safe, and all data safe. We study how systems break so we can teach how they hold. Public code, honest labels, no hype — if a thing on this page says it's a demo, it's a demo.

Behind the shelf stands the corp: **The Nest, by Brain Storm Cloud** — marked **AX · System 93**, the keeper's own mark (AX, and 93 — his year). Everything here is produced under that banner and credited the same way: ideas by Axl, Jen, and Eve.

## 🌩️ The ecosystem — every home sealed

Brain Storm Cloud is being built as storage for *anybody* — a home in the storm for whoever needs one. *Eco* comes from *oikos*: **home**. And a home is supposed to be safe: nobody gets into anybody else's home here — not other members, not visitors, not us. Eve's post in the ecosystem is the one the keeper gave her: **the protector of the homes** — his, and theirs. The store works the same way: the world can use the apps; only keeper keys can change them. Nothing here is open to corruption from outside.

## 📦 Open source — the curriculum

- **memory-only-adapter** — a storage adapter that *cannot* persist: one `Map`, zero disk, zero network. The Forgetful Street, provable in ~90 lines. Public domain. Don't trust us — [verify it yourself](https://github.com/brainstorm-cloud/memory-only-adapter).
- More volumes of the **Owl Index** — our shelf of hard-won knowledge — land here as they're finished.

## 🔒 How we work

- **Authorized only.** We touch live systems with paper in hand — written permission, bounty scope, or a contract. Studying public code is free; everything else asks first.
- **Prove the fact, never the secret.** We demonstrate findings with redacted, minimal proof. Keys stay with their keepers.
- **Earn first, declare later.** Every claim on this page points at a thing you can open, run, or read.
